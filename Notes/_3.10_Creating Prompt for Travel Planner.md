### Learning Objectives

---

## 1. Travel Planner Prompt

A **Travel Planner Prompt** is a prompt designed to instruct AI to create a travel plan based on requirements such as:

* Destination
* Duration
* Budget
* Number of travelers
* Interests
* Transportation
* Accommodation
* Daily activities

The quality of the travel plan depends on how clearly these requirements are specified.

---

## 2. Basic Travel Planning Prompt

### Simple Prompt

```text
Plan a trip to Goa.
```

This does not provide enough information for a useful personalized plan.

### Improved Prompt

```text
Create a 3-day travel plan for Goa for 2 students.

Include:
- Places to visit
- Activities
- Food suggestions
- Approximate daily schedule

Keep the plan budget-friendly.
Present the result day-wise in a table.
```

---

## 3. Travel Planner Using RTCCO

### Role

Define the role of the AI.

```text
Act as an experienced travel planner.
```

### Task

Clearly define what needs to be created.

```text
Create a 4-day travel itinerary for Jaipur.
```

### Context

Provide relevant travel information.

```text
The travelers are two college students visiting Jaipur
for the first time.
```

### Constraints

Specify requirements and limitations.

```text
Keep the total budget within ₹15,000 excluding travel to Jaipur.
Prefer affordable accommodation and local transportation.
```

### Output Format

Specify how the result should be presented.

```text
Present the itinerary in a table with:
Day, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Estimated Cost.
```

---

## 4. Complete Travel Planner Prompt

```text
Act as an experienced travel planner.

Create a 4-day travel itinerary for Jaipur for two college
students visiting for the first time.

Requirements:
- Include major tourist attractions.
- Include local food experiences.
- Prefer affordable transportation.
- Suggest budget-friendly activities.
- Keep the local trip budget within ₹15,000.
- Avoid an overly packed schedule.

Present the itinerary in a table with the following columns:
Day | Morning | Afternoon | Evening | Estimated Cost

Also provide a brief summary of the estimated total cost.
```

## 5. Adding More Specific Requirements

A travel prompt can be optimized by providing specific requirements.

### Example

```text
Act as a travel planner.

Plan a 5-day trip to Kerala for 4 people.

Context:
- First-time visitors
- Interested in nature, local food and cultural experiences

Constraints:
- Moderate budget
- Maximum 3 major activities per day
- Include approximate travel time between locations
- Avoid unnecessary backtracking

Output:
1. Day-wise itinerary
2. Estimated daily cost
3. Transportation suggestions
4. Important travel tips

Present the itinerary using tables and bullet points.
```

| Component      | Example                          |

## 7. Prompt Optimization for Travel Planning

### Initial Prompt

```text
Plan a trip to Mumbai.
```

### Optimized Prompt

```text
Act as a travel planner.

Create a 2-day Mumbai itinerary for 3 college students.

Context:
- First-time visitors
- Interested in historical places, street food and popular attractions

Constraints:
- Budget-friendly
- Use local transportation where practical
- Maximum 4 major activities per day

Output:
Create a day-wise table containing:
Time | Place | Activity | Estimated Cost

End with the estimated total local expenditure.
```

The optimized prompt gives the AI clear information about **who is travelling, where they are going, what they want, their limitations, and how the answer should be presented**.

---

## Best Practices

* Clearly specify the **destination and duration**.
* Mention the **number of travelers**.
* Provide the **budget** when cost matters.
* Mention interests and preferences.
* Specify transportation requirements if relevant.
* Avoid unrealistic numbers of activities in one day.
* Specify the desired output format.
* Use constraints to make the itinerary more practical.

> **Note:** AI-generated travel information, prices, opening hours, transportation schedules, and availability should be verified before actual travel.
